# Speedometer Digital Support

Speedometer Digital is a privacy-first GPS speedometer and trip tracker for
iPhone and iPad.

## Getting started

1. Open Speedometer Digital and allow location access while using the app.
2. Choose kilometres per hour, miles per hour, or metres per second in
   **Settings**.
3. Tap **Start Trip** to record a route and trip statistics.
4. Use **Map** for your live route and **Trips** to review or delete saved
   journeys.
5. Open **HUD** from the dashboard for a focused, high-contrast speed display.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is no speed displayed?

Move to a location with a clear view of the sky and confirm that Location
Services are enabled for Speedometer Digital. GPS accuracy can be reduced
indoors, in tunnels, near tall buildings, or when the device has limited
satellite visibility.

### Why does the app ask for location access?

Location is needed to calculate speed, heading, distance, and trip routes. The
developer does not receive your location or trip history. See the
[Privacy Policy](../).

### Where are my trips stored?

Trips are stored locally on your device. Speedometer Digital does not provide
an online account or cloud sync.

### Can I prevent background location use?

The app uses background location only while a trip recording is active, so a
journey can continue when the screen locks or another app is opened. Stop the
trip when you no longer want it recorded. iOS shows the standard location
indicator while background access is active.

### How do I delete a trip?

Open **Trips**, select the saved journey, and use the delete action. Deleting
the app removes all locally stored trips and preferences from the device,
subject to the device's normal backup and restore behaviour.

### Is the displayed speed guaranteed to match my vehicle?

No. GPS readings can lag or become less accurate because of satellite
conditions, device placement, and signal quality. Use your vehicle's
instruments and obey local road rules. Do not interact with the app while
driving.

## Accessibility

The app supports system text sizing, VoiceOver labels, dark appearance, reduced
motion, and high-contrast speed displays. Read the
[Accessibility Statement](../accessibility/) for details.
